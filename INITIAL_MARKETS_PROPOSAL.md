# Initial Launch Market Proposal (4.7.1, 4.8, and beyond)

This document proposes initial markets for AstroLedger launch, focused on what happens after Alpha 4.7: the 4.7.1 follow-up, the Alpha 4.8 feature slate, and the next visible roadmap beats beyond that. The goal is a launch lineup with stronger hooks, cleaner resolution criteria, and more variety in market type.

## Notes

- Based on the live Alpha 4.7 patch notes, the March 25, 2026 Roadmap Roundup, the current 4.8 tentative cards, and public upcoming-features tracking for 4.7.1.
- Prioritize engaging markets around hotfix cadence, 4.7.1 content, 4.8 feature delivery, economy normalization, and roadmap migration.
- Avoid low-signal questions that are too tied to 4.7 launch-day conditions or distorted by the current economy imbalance.
- Sort markets into Boolean, Time Frame, and Numeric / Multiple Choice so the launch slate has better variety.

## Source signals worth using

- 4.7.x still has unresolved pressure points from live: inventory quirks, stack-all disabled, freight-elevator friction, Savrilium price normalization, and other known issues.
- The March 25 roadmap explicitly marks Nyx Missions (Pack 2) as planned for a later 4.7.x release.
- The same roadmap puts several 4.8 cards in Tentative, including Refueling Missions, Refueling Improvements, Ship Hangar Service T0, Transport System, Hammerhead Gold Standard, and new Wikelo offerings.
- Public upcoming-features tracking currently points to Hull B and UTV as 4.7.1 targets, and the Novia Crossbow as a 4.8 target.

## Market framing guidance

- Favor markets that resolve from official patch notes, roadmap updates, in-game availability, or easily documented public announcements.
- Prefer concrete nouns and patch names over vague sentiment language.
- Use time-frame and count markets to make patch-following more fun than plain yes/no.
- Keep a few spicier markets, but make sure they still resolve cleanly.
